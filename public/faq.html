Frequently Asked Questions

Can't find what you need? Contact us directly.

Orders & Payment
What payment methods do you accept?

Cash on Delivery (COD) only, nationwide. You pay the rider when your order arrives — nothing is charged upfront. Card payments are coming soon.

Can I change or cancel my order after placing it?

Contact us as soon as possible with your order number. We can usually adjust or cancel an order before it ships, but once it's out for delivery we can't intercept it.

Do you accept promo codes?

Yes — enter your code at checkout or in the cart, and the discount is validated and applied automatically before you pay.

Shipping & Delivery
How long does delivery take?

3–5 working days in major cities, 5–7 elsewhere in Pakistan. You'll see an estimate on every product page and again at checkout.

Is shipping free?

Yes, on orders over Rs. 5,000. Below that, a flat Rs. 250 shipping fee applies.

How do I track my order?

Use our order tracking page with your order number and the email you used at checkout.

Sizing & Fit
How do I know what size to order?

Every product page lists available sizes with live stock, and jeans with a waist × length option let you pick both dimensions separately. If you're between sizes, our general rule: size up for a relaxed fit, true-to-size for slim/tapered cuts.

What if my size is out of stock?

Sizes marked out of stock will be restocked when possible — check back, or contact us to ask about restock timing on a specific item.

Do your jeans shrink after washing?

Our denim is pre-washed/sanforized, so shrinkage after the first wash is minimal when you follow the care instructions on each product page.

Returns & Exchanges
What's your return policy?

Free exchange within 14 days of delivery, provided the item is unworn with tags attached. See our full Refund & Returns Policy for details.

How do I start a return or exchange?

Contact us with your order number and we'll walk you through pickup and the exchange.

Account & Reviews
Do I need an account to order?

No — guest checkout is available on every order. Creating an account just saves your addresses and order history for next time.

Can I leave a review?

Yes, on any product you've purchased — log in, open the product page, and use the "Write a Review" section. Verified purchases are marked automatically.
