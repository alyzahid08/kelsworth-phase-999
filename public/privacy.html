Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2026

This policy explains what information Kelsworth collects when you use our website, and how we use it.

Information We Collect

When you browse, create an account, or place an order, we may collect:

  • Contact details — name, email, phone number, shipping address
  • Order information — items purchased, sizes, order history
  • Account information — if you create an account, a hashed password, saved addresses, wishlist, and preferences
  • Device information — basic technical data like browser type, used to keep the site working correctly

Recently viewed items and cart contents are stored locally in your browser, not linked to your identity unless you're logged in.

How We Use Your Information

  • To process and deliver your orders
  • To send order confirmations and delivery updates
  • To respond to customer service requests
  • To send marketing emails, only if you've opted in via notification preferences in your account
  • To improve our products and website

How We Protect Your Information

Passwords are stored using industry-standard one-way hashing — we never store your password in plain text. Account sessions use secure, signed cookies. We don't sell or rent your personal information to third parties.

Payment Information

All Kelsworth orders are Cash on Delivery — we do not collect or store any card or bank payment details on our website.

Your Choices

  • Update or delete saved addresses any time from Your Account
  • Turn marketing emails on or off under Account → Profile → Notification Preferences
  • Request a copy or deletion of your data by contacting us

Cookies & Local Storage

We use your browser's local storage to remember your cart, wishlist-adjacent browsing history, and recently viewed items — this data stays on your device and isn't sent to our servers unless it's part of an action you take, like adding an item to your cart at checkout.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected with an updated "last updated" date above.
