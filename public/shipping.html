Shipping & Delivery

Nationwide delivery with Cash on Delivery — here's exactly what to expect.

Delivery Timelines

  • Major cities (Karachi, Lahore, Islamabad, Rawalpindi, Faisalabad): 3–5 working days
  • Everywhere else in Pakistan: 5–7 working days

Orders are processed within 1–2 business days before handing off to our courier partner. You'll see the same estimate on every product page and again at checkout, based on your delivery city.

Shipping Rates

  • Free shipping on all orders over Rs. 5,000
  • Rs. 250 flat rate on orders below that

Your cart shows a live progress bar toward free shipping, and the exact shipping cost is confirmed again at checkout before you place your order.

Cash on Delivery

Every order ships Cash on Delivery — you pay the courier in cash when your package arrives, not before. We don't currently support online card payments, but it's on our roadmap.

Order Tracking

Once your order is placed, you can check its status any time on our order tracking page using your order number and the email address you checked out with. If you have an account, your order history is also visible under Your Account.

Delivery Issues

If your order is delayed, arrives damaged, or doesn't show up, contact us with your order number and we'll sort it out — including a reship or refund where appropriate.
