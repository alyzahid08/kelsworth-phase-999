Refund & Returns Policy

Last updated: July 2026

14-Day Exchange Window

We offer free exchanges within 14 days of delivery. To qualify, an item must be:

  • Unworn, unwashed, and undamaged
  • In its original packaging with tags still attached
  • Accompanied by your order number

How to Request an Exchange

  1. Contact us with your order number and the item you'd like to exchange.
  2. We'll confirm eligibility and arrange pickup of the item.
  3. Once received and inspected, we'll ship your replacement size or item.

Refunds

Since all orders are paid via Cash on Delivery, refunds for returned items are issued as store credit or bank transfer, whichever you prefer — we don't hold any card payment to reverse. Refunds are processed within 5–7 working days of us receiving the returned item.

What Isn't Eligible

  • Items marked "Final Sale"
  • Items returned after 14 days of delivery
  • Items showing signs of wear, washing, or damage not caused by us

Damaged or Incorrect Items

If an item arrives damaged or isn't what you ordered, this isn't subject to the standard exchange conditions above — contact us right away and we'll make it right at no cost to you.

Questions

Reach out any time via our contact page — we handle every return personally.
