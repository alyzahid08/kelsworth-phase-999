About Kelsworth

Denim and workwear built for people who wear the same jacket for ten years, not ten washes.

Our Story

Kelsworth started in Karachi with a simple frustration: most "premium" denim sold here was neither. We wanted heavyweight cotton that actually held its shape, stitching that didn't come undone after a season, and sizing you could trust without a fitting room. So we started making our own.

Every piece is developed in-house — fabric weight, wash, and fit are chosen for how they wear in, not just how they photograph. We'd rather sell you one jacket that lasts five years than five jackets that don't.

What We Believe

Fabric first

We spec our own denim weights and cotton blends rather than buying whatever's cheapest that season. If a fabric doesn't hold a crease or survive a hundred washes, it doesn't make the cut.

Honest sizing

Every product page lists real fabric composition, fit notes, and stock down to the size — no guessing games, no "true to size" without the detail to back it up.

No pressure payment

Cash on Delivery, nationwide. You don't pay a rupee until the order is in your hands.
