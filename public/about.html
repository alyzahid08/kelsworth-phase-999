About AL-QUTUZ

القطز — a premium men's fabric house built on Wash & Wear and Cotton unstitched lengths, cut for distinction and finished to hold their shape.

A Name Built on Resolve

AL-QUTUZ takes its name from a historical figure remembered for resolve under pressure — a namesake we carry lightly, as a point of character rather than costume. We're not a history brand and we don't claim to be; the name is simply a reminder of the standard we hold our fabric to: steady, considered, built to last.

That discipline shows up in what we choose to sell. Wash & Wear is our signature — wrinkle-resistant unstitched lengths that go from wash to wardrobe with barely a crease. Cotton sits alongside it for the hotter months, and a small, curated edit of unstitched Wash & Wear and Cotton lengths rounds out the wardrobe without competing for attention.
